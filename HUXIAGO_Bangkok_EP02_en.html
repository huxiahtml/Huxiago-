Across Bangkok

0202 · Stay

SILQ Hotel & Residence Bangkok

A night in Phrom Phong

⌄

The spacious Chiffon Studio, breakfast and private onsen stood out during Xiao Hu’s hosted stay.

Good to knowThe hotel is on Sukhumvit Soi 24. Rates vary by date and package. Ask the hotel about private onsen access.

0303 · Street food

Gan Da · กานดาอาหารตามสั่ง

Pad kra pao straight from the wok

⌄

Xiao Hu often orders minced pork pad kra pao over rice with a runny fried egg. If there are two of you, consider sharing a bowl of tom yum.

Good to knowLook near Central Lat Phrao. Ask for your preferred spice level when ordering. The kitchen may close early if it sells out.

04 · Wellness

Hani Spa · Branch 2

An hour off your feet

⌄

Xiao Hu and Kik visited Branch 2. Thai massage and foot massage both make for a short break. Tell the therapist what pressure you prefer.

Good to knowThe source approaches Lat Phrao Soi 1 from MRT Phahon Yothin. Select Branch 2 and confirm the treatment, price and appointment directly.
↗ Open Google Maps
0505 · Coffee & desserts

Chowchercof Cafe

Cake by a lotus pond

⌄

Xiao Hu’s usual is the Coco Ball coconut pandan cake. The matcha and butterfly pea mousse is another pick. Leave yourself a moment to enjoy the pond.

Good to knowThe source suggests walking from MRT Lat Phrao. Check opening hours and dessert availability before you go.
↗ Open Google Maps
0606 · Shopping

Bang Sue Junction

Vintage finds in the Red Building

⌄

Xiao Hu browsed antiques on the lower floors, then went upstairs for vintage clothes, records and snacks. Individual shops keep their own hours.

Good to knowThe source walks from MRT Kamphaeng Phet. A little cash is handy for secondhand shops and snacks.
↗ Open Google Maps
0707 · Transport

Khlong Saen Saep Express Boat

Eastbound from Pratunam

⌄

Xiao Hu and Kik boarded at 16:48 and reached The Mall Bangkapi at 17:38. Their eastbound ride took 50 minutes and got crowded around rush hour.

West to east · selected piers

  1. Saphan Hua Chang
  2. Pratunam
  3. Asoke-Petchaburi
  4. Soi Thonglor
  5. Mahadthai
  6. The Mall Bangkapi
Good to knowFares depend on distance and current notices. Xiao Hu paid in baht on board. If you take the boat to a concert, arrange a way back before you leave.
