## 繁體中文

### 穿梭曼谷市中心的快艇｜盛桑運河快船

想避開曼谷市中心的車陣，可以試試沿運河行駛的盛桑運河快船。市中心只搭幾站，花費約 20 泰銖，有些短程不到 20 分鐘。小胡這次搭乘時，覺得船和碼頭有些年頭，沒聞到明顯的運河異味，倒是柴油味比較有感。沿途看見有人跑步、聊天和晾衣服，也能從靠船頭的位置看看船長怎麼開船。

星期三傍晚，小胡和 Kik 在 16:48 從水門碼頭上船，17:38 到 The Mall Bangkapi。這趟往東的長程搭了 50 分鐘，越接近下班時間，船上越擠，Kik 幾乎一路站著。通勤時段可以把快船列為備選，也要考慮人潮。

由西往東，可以留意這六個碼頭。中間還有其他停靠站，上船前先確認方向和目的地。

- **Saphan Hua Chang**：步行可到暹羅商圈或 MBK。
- **Pratunam**：靠近水門市場，原文估計從 CentralWorld 步行約 5 分鐘。
- **Asoke-Petchaburi**：可轉 MRT Phetchaburi 或機場快線 Makkasan。若要去 BTS Asok 或 Terminal 21，預留步行或轉乘時間。
- **Soi Thonglor**：前往通羅、Ekkamai 一帶可留意。
- **Mahadthai**：去 Rajamangala National Stadium 看演唱會時，可依活動入口規劃從碼頭步行的路線。
- **The Mall Bangkapi**：可接 MRT 黃線，也是小胡這趟的目的地。

上船後站穩或坐好，等票務人員來收費，再告訴對方下船碼頭。小胡這次用泰銖現金付款，準備零錢或小額紙鈔比較方便。原文參考票價約 16–26 泰銖，從水門到 Asoke-Petchaburi 約 18 泰銖，到 The Mall Bangkapi 約 26 泰銖。

搭船去看演唱會時，先安排好散場後的交通。末班時間依日期、方向和路段而異，晚間出發前再核對當天船班。原文提到的 Saen Saeb Canal 互動地圖可供查詢，正式連結待確認。

## English

### Across Central Bangkok by Canal Boat | Khlong Saen Saep Express Boat

The Khlong Saen Saep Express Boat offers a way around central Bangkok’s traffic. A few stops downtown cost around 20 baht, and some short trips take under 20 minutes. On Xiao Hu’s ride, the boats and piers showed their age, with diesel fumes more noticeable than any smell from the canal. Along the banks, people jogged, chatted and hung out their laundry. From near the bow, you could watch the captain handle the boat.

On a Wednesday afternoon, Xiao Hu and Kik boarded at Pratunam at 4:48 p.m. and reached The Mall Bangkapi at 5:38 p.m. The longer eastbound ride took 50 minutes and got more crowded as rush hour set in. Kik stood for almost the whole journey. The boat is an option during rush hour, especially if you’re prepared for the crowds.

Here are six useful piers in west-to-east order. Other stops lie between them, so check your destination and direction before boarding.

- **Saphan Hua Chang** is within walking distance of the Siam shopping district and MBK.
- **Pratunam** is near Pratunam Market. The original account puts it about a five-minute walk from CentralWorld.
- **Asoke-Petchaburi** connects to MRT Phetchaburi and the Airport Rail Link at Makkasan. Allow extra walking or transfer time for BTS Asok or Terminal 21.
- **Soi Thonglor** is a stop to consider for Thonglor and Ekkamai.
- **Mahadthai** is an option for concerts at Rajamangala National Stadium. Plan the walk from the pier around your event entrance.
- **The Mall Bangkapi**, where Xiao Hu got off, connects to the MRT Yellow Line.

Once you’re safely seated or standing, wait for the fare collector and say which pier you’re going to. Xiao Hu paid in baht, and coins or small notes are handy. The source gives a fare range of about 16–26 baht, with Pratunam to Asoke-Petchaburi at about 18 baht and Pratunam to The Mall Bangkapi at about 26 baht.

If you take the boat to a concert, plan your trip back in advance. Last departures vary by date, direction and section of the route, so check that day’s service before an evening trip.
